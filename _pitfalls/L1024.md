---
layout: pitfall
title: "math.log10() and math.mod() removed in Lua 5.2"
permalink: /p/L1024/
description: >-
  `math.log10()` was deprecated in Lua 5.2 and removed in Lua 5.3; `math.mod()`
  was already deprecated in Lua 5.1 and removed in Lua 5.2. Use `math.log(x, 10)`
  for base-10 logarithms and the `%` operator (or `math.fmod()`) for modulo.
runtime:
  - Lua 5.2
  - Lua 5.3
  - Lua 5.4
  - Lua 5.5
analyzers:
  - luacheck
  - selene
---

```lua
print(math.log10(1000))   -- 3 (Lua 5.1, deprecated in 5.2)
print(math.mod(7, 3))     -- 1 (Lua 5.1 only)
```

Portable forms:

```lua
print(math.log(1000, 10))   -- 3
print(7 % 3)                -- 1
print(math.fmod(7, 3))      -- 1
```

`luacheck` and `selene` flag these as undefined fields on the corresponding
versions.
